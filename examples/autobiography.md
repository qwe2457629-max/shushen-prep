# 自傳 Autobiography

## 中文

我是林小安，目前就讀 OO大學 XX學系四年級。我在彰化長大，家裡開一間小型的五金行，從國中開始，寒暑假都在店裡幫忙顧店、對帳。那時候最常做的事，是每個月底跟爸爸一起把進貨單和電費單攤在桌上，看哪裡花太多。這個習慣一直跟著我：遇到問題，先把數字找出來。

大學前兩年，我花最多時間在淨灘社。大二當活動長，一學期辦了 6 場淨灘，總共約 180 人次參加。辦活動讓我學到一件事：把垃圾撿起來很容易，難的是讓大家下次不要再丟。所以我們後來每場都記錄撿到的垃圾種類，發現 4 成是飲料杯和吸管，就跟學校附近 3 家飲料店談，讓自備杯的同學多折 5 元。

大三的專題和暑期實習，讓我把興趣從「撿垃圾」轉到「省能源」。我發現建築用電的資料其實很多，只是很少人去看。我希望在研究所學會更扎實的分析方法，也學會怎麼把分析結果變成大家願意執行的方案。

## English

I am Lin Hsiao-An, a senior in the Department of XX at OO University. I grew up in Changhua, where my family runs a small hardware store. Since junior high, I have spent school breaks working at the counter and checking accounts. Every month my father and I would spread the purchase orders and electricity bills on the table to see where we were overspending. That habit stayed with me: when something goes wrong, I look for the numbers first.

In my first two years of college, most of my free time went to the beach cleanup club. As events lead in my second year, I organized six cleanups in one semester with about 180 participants in total. I learned that picking up trash is easy; getting people not to drop it again is hard. We started logging what we collected and found that 40% was drink cups and straws, so we persuaded three nearby drink shops to give students a NT$5 discount for bringing their own cups.

My capstone project and summer internship moved my interest from litter to energy. Buildings produce plenty of electricity data, but few people read it. In graduate school I want to build stronger analytical skills and learn how to turn analysis into plans that people will actually carry out.
